## Email Send Times (Research-Based)

**Optimal Send Windows:**
- **Primary:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am (10am is the single best hour)
- **Secondary:** Early morning (7:30–8:30am) or end-of-day (3:30–4:30pm) for school staff
- **Avoid:** Monday (admin catch-up), Friday (disengagement), weekends (B2B volume drops)

**All sends scheduled for 10:00am UTC (mid-morning mid-week)**

**Why late August is NOT too late:**
- UK schools start Sept 1 (most), making late Aug prime for last-minute planning
- Budget cycles: fiscal-year budgets (July 1) active, procurement windows open
- Late-shopper purchasing continues through Aug/Sept
- "Now that term has started" service messaging is high-converting
