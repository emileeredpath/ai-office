## PPC Strategy

### Campaign Segmentation

**By Product Category:**
1. **Two-Way Radios** — Target: Radio professionals, businesses needing on-site communication
2. **CCTV & Access Control** — Target: Security managers, facilities managers, retail businesses
3. **Drones** — Target: Site managers, inspectors, professionals needing aerial services
4. **Audio Visual** — Target: Event managers, corporate AV teams, venues
5. **MTech AI** — Target: Businesses with existing Motorola radio networks

## Monitoring & Optimization

### Weekly Monitoring (Aug onwards)
- Check all campaigns daily for spend anomalies
- Monitor CTR trends (alert if drops >10%)
- Track CPC changes (alert if increases >15%)
- Review new queries added to negative keywords

### Bi-weekly Optimization
- Pause underperforming ad variants
- Launch new ad copy tests
- Adjust bids based on performance
- Review search term reports for new keywords

### Monthly Reviews
- Full campaign performance analysis
- ROAS calculation by campaign
- Keyword performance deep-dive
- Budget reallocation if needed
- Report to stakeholders

## Conversion Tracking

### Setup Required
1. ✅ Google Analytics 4 (GA4) connected
2. ✅ Conversion goals defined (form submission, phone call, page view)
3. ✅ UTM parameters on all landing pages
4. ✅ Google Ads conversion tracking enabled
5. ✅ Cross-domain tracking for product pages

### Tracking Method
- **Primary:** Form submissions (inquiry forms on product pages)
- **Secondary:** Phone calls (call tracking number)
- **Tertiary:** GA4 user segment (high-value visitors)

### Validation
- Weekly reconciliation of conversions between GA4 and Google Ads
- Monthly cross-check with CRM lead imports
- Alert system for tracking drops >20%
