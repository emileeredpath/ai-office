- "professional video recorder"

**Secondary Keywords (Mid intent):**

**Tertiary Keywords (Awareness):**

## Monitoring & Optimization

### Daily Monitoring (Sep 1-15)
- Check spend vs budget (daily limit alerts)
- Monitor CTR (alert if drops below 1.2%)
- Track CPC changes (alert if exceeds £1.50)
- Watch conversion data from landing pages
- Review new search queries for negatives

### Weekly Optimization (Sep 1+)
- Pause underperforming ad variants
- Adjust bids on high-performing keywords
- Add negative keywords from search terms
- Optimize landing page copy based on behavior
- Report weekly performance to stakeholders

### Bi-weekly Reviews (Sep 8, 15)
- Full performance analysis vs targets
- Keyword performance breakdown
- Entity-specific performance comparison
- Landing page optimization recommendations
- Budget reallocation if needed

### Monthly Review (Sep 30)
- Final performance vs goals
- Cost per lead analysis
- Lead quality assessment
- Campaign learnings for Q4
- Recommendations for scaling or pausing

## Conversion Tracking

### Setup Required
1. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) connected to all 4 landing pages
2. Conversion goals: Lead form submission, phone call
3. UTM parameters: source=google_ads, medium=cpc, campaign=axon_bodycam, content=[entity]
4. Google Ads conversion tracking enabled
5. Lead form submission events tracked in GA4

### Validation
- Test form submissions before Sep 1
- Daily reconciliation of conversions Sep 1-8
- Weekly reporting of form submissions by entity
- CRM import validation (leads should appear in CRM within 24 hours)

### Reporting
- Daily: Spend, impressions, clicks, CTR
- Weekly: Leads generated, cost per lead, lead quality
- Monthly: Full campaign analysis, ROI projection
