## Email Content

### Subject Line
"Meet Your New Account Manager — [Brand Name]"

### Email Structure

**Header:** Brand logo + "Introducing Your Account Manager"

**Opening:** 
"We're excited to introduce [Account Manager Name], your new dedicated Account Manager at [Brand Name]. [Name] is here to help you get the most out of your communication systems and support your team's needs."

**Body:**
- Account Manager bio (50-75 words)
- Photo (professional headshot)
- Contact details: email, phone, direct messaging preference
- Key responsibilities (support, updates, opportunities)
- Invitation to connect: "Reach out anytime with questions or ideas"

**CTA:** "Connect with [Name]" button to email or booking link

**Closing:** Brand-specific sign-off + company contact info
